ChartRemotely — say it here, see it there

Put a security on a desktop chart anywhere in the world: a wall monitor in another building, a screen you left behind. Say “Palantir” to Siri and the thinkorswim chart on your Mac changes. Then see a live snapshot of it from any browser.

How it works

  • A small agent runs on the Mac driving the display. It dials out, so nothing on your network is exposed.
  • You pair that Mac to your Nostr identity with a short code.
  • You speak to an Apple Shortcut, or ask any AI assistant, and the chart changes.

What it cannot do

It cannot open an order ticket, trade, move money or read an account. The agent enforces a short fixed vocabulary: show a security, change the time frame, report what is shown, take a snapshot.

For AI assistants

The same tools are an MCP server at https://chartremotely-mcp.fastmcp.app/mcp (streamable HTTP). Sign-in is a Nostr npub proof; requests are paid from a prepaid Bitcoin Lightning balance.
